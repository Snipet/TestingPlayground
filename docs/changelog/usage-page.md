### A page that shows what the chat has used and cost

**Added**
- **Settings → Usage**: what the chat's requests have used and cost, from the usage file. Choose a range (7 days, 30
  days, 12 months, All) and Tokens or Cost; a chart stacks a bar a day (a month in the longer ranges) by model, the
  busiest a colour each and the rest Other, and the bar under the pointer — or picked with the arrow keys — is read out
  below it. A table lists a row a model (its provider, requests, tokens in, cached, out, cost and share) and a total.
- A cost with ~ is an estimate at the prices in the footnote (dated as the price table is); one without is the
  provider's own figure; "no price known" where there is none; your provider's invoice is the record. Reset… forgets
  every request counted (asked first). Nothing of a chat is read or shown.
